A short trip written the way this skill asks for: the to-do list first, then the days, then a fallback that is still live. Names, times and prices here are illustrative; look everything up for a real plan.

Note what is not here. No comparison of Nikko against the places that lost, no table of the costs of the plan, no list of sources, no record of the timetable error that was corrected on the way. The detail that matters sits on the stop line of the day that needs it.

# Nikko, two days

Two nights, back in Tokyo for a 20:00 dinner on 9/18. No car: Tobu train and Tobu bus only.
Booked: Revaty Kegon 47, Asakusa 13:00 to Tobu-Nikko 14:50 on 9/16, two reserved seats.

## To do

### Book now

- [ ] **Ryokan at Yumoto, 9/17, dinner included.** The only night with no bed. Ten inns in the whole village and most are half board. Free cancellation until 9/10
- [ ] Nikko Station Hotel Classic, 9/16, one twin. Check-in runs to 23:30, put that in the booking note

### Buy or pack before you go

- [ ] Bear bell. Sightings this summer are on the Senjogahara boardwalk, the stretch you walk on 9/17
- [ ] Rain gear. September is the wettest month up here

### On the ground

- [ ] Nikko All Area Pass, two days, 4,780 yen, at Asakusa on the morning of 9/16. Not sold online
- [ ] 1,600 yen cash for the Toshogu ticket

### Recheck before you go

- [ ] Akanuma boardwalk after the September rains, park site, the night before
- [ ] Toshogu closing time, the 9/16 afternoon is tight if it moved

## 2026-09-16 Wed Arrive in Nikko

- 15:40 🚆 [Tobu-Nikko Station](geo:36.7476,139.6189) Revaty Kegon from Asakusa, seat reserved
- 🚶 [Shinkyo Bridge](geo:36.7546,139.6033) the red bridge at the entrance to the shrines

    Bring 1,600 yen cash for the ticket

    Most restaurants in town close by 19:00; the hotel restaurant takes last orders at 20:00
    Two 24 hour convenience stores about 450m from the station if you arrive too late

## 2026-09-17 Thu Up to Senjogahara, sleep at Yumoto

Bear bell on the boardwalk, and do not walk at the front or the back alone. Falls to lake is downhill in this direction, which is why it runs this way round.

1. 07:53 🚌 [Yudaki Falls](geo:36.7959,139.4285) Tobu bus from stop 2A, two-day pass covers it
    Buy the pass before boarding. The cheaper Chuzenji pass does not reach Yumoto

    Closed sometimes after rain, check the park site the night before
3. 12:52 🚌 [Kegon Falls](geo:36.7380,139.5020) elevator to the base, 570 yen, last elevator 17:00
4. 🚶 [Chuzenji Lake](geo:36.7368,139.4769) lakeside walk if legs allow
5. 17:20 🚌 ♨️ [Yumoto Onsen](geo:36.8068,139.4231) soak, dinner at the ryokan at 18:30
    Later buses 17:55 and 18:55, the last one in. About 12C at night, bring long sleeves

## Fallback: Karuizawa if the forecast turns

Decide on the 9/14 forecast, while the 9/16 train tickets are still refundable. Karuizawa is 2.8C warmer but half the rain and everything is indoors-capable, and one hotel covers both nights. Hokuriku Shinkansen from Ueno, 70 minutes. Buy dinner at Tokyo Station either way: the town has no 24 hour shop.
