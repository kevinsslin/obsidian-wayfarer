# Example: a finished note

A short trip written the way this skill asks for: the itinerary the plugin reads, then the planning notes behind it. Names, times and prices here are illustrative; look everything up for a real plan.

```markdown
# Nikko, two days

## 2026-09-16 Wed Arrive in Nikko

- 15:40 🚆 [Tobu-Nikko Station](geo:36.7476,139.6189) Revaty Kegon from Asakusa, seat reserved
- 🚶 [Shinkyo Bridge](geo:36.7546,139.6033) the red bridge at the entrance to the shrines
- ⛩️ [Nikko Toshogu](geo:36.7580,139.5990) last entry 16:30
    Sleeping Cat and the Three Wise Monkeys carvings
    Bring 1,600 yen cash for the ticket
- 🚌 🏨 [Nikko Station Hotel Classic](geo:36.7469,139.6215) check in, dinner nearby
    Most restaurants in town close by 19:00; the hotel restaurant takes last orders at 20:00

## 2026-09-17 Thu Up to Senjogahara, sleep at Yumoto

1. 07:53 🚌 [Yudaki Falls](geo:36.7959,139.4285) Tobu bus from stop 2A, two-day pass covers it
2. 🚶 [Akanuma](geo:36.7710,139.4558) boardwalk across the marsh, about 1h45
    Boardwalk sometimes closed after rain, check the park site the night before
3. 12:52 🚌 [Kegon Falls](geo:36.7380,139.5020) elevator to the base, 570 yen, last elevator 17:00
4. 🚶 [Chuzenji Lake](geo:36.7368,139.4769) lakeside walk if legs allow
5. 17:20 🚌 ♨️ [Yumoto Onsen](geo:36.8068,139.4231) soak, dinner at the ryokan at 18:30

## Hard constraints

- Two nights only: back in Tokyo by the evening of 9/18 for a 20:00 dinner.
- No car. Everything by Tobu train and Tobu bus.
- One person walks slowly on descents; no more than one long walk per day.
- Budget for the two days including hotels: 60,000 yen for two people.

## Bookings

Confirmed
- Revaty Kegon 47, Asakusa 13:00 to Tobu-Nikko 14:50, 9/16, two reserved seats, 5,760 yen.
- Nikko Station Hotel Classic, 9/16, one twin, breakfast included, 18,000 yen.

To book
- Yumoto ryokan, 9/17, dinner included. Two candidates in the open questions; decide by 9/10 when cancellation is still free.
- Nikko All Area Pass (two days, 4,780 yen) at Asakusa on the morning of 9/16. Not sold online.

## Sep 16 to 17: mountain, not the coast

Decision: Nikko and Oku-Nikko, two nights split between the town and the lake.

Why: the shrines close at 17:00 and the lake buses thin out after 17:30, so a single base means either an early shrine rush or a late return. Sleeping in town the first night gives the shrines a full afternoon; sleeping at Yumoto the second night means the 17:20 bus is the last transfer of the day and the onsen is the reward.

Costs: the two-day pass pays for itself on 9/17 alone (Tobu-Nikko to Yumoto is 1,950 yen each way). The split means packing up twice.

Backup: Karuizawa (Hokuriku Shinkansen from Ueno, 70 minutes) if a typhoon is tracking toward Tochigi on 9/14. It is flatter and everything is indoors-capable, but the 9/16 train tickets are non-refundable after 9/15, so the switch has to happen on the 9/14 forecast.

Sources: Tobu bus timetable (2026 summer edition), Toshogu official hours page, national park boardwalk notice board.

## Open questions

- [ ] Ryokan at Yumoto: the one with the outdoor bath or the cheaper one by the bus stop
- [ ] Is the Akanuma boardwalk open after the September rains? Check the park site on 9/16
- [ ] Ryuzu Falls on the way down if time allows on 9/17
```
